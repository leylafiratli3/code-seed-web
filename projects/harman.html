The brief

Harman needed to automate complex allocation decisions across regions and time zones, track inventory in real time across global warehouses, and connect several regional ERP systems, all while processing sensitive financial data at high volume.

Our approach

We built a cloud operations platform around an allocation engine that makes and optimizes those decisions automatically, with machine-learning forecasts six months ahead and a high-throughput order pipeline. A custom integration layer ties regional SAP and TOTVS systems into a single Snowflake warehouse.

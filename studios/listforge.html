THE PROBLEM

Army lists are a chore

Tabletop players needed a faster, more reliable way to build and manage their army lists. Spreadsheets and paper were slow, and point-calculation errors were common.

We needed something that could handle complex game rules and still feel easy for both casual and competitive players.

WHAT WE BUILT

A list builder that keeps up

ListForge makes building accurate lists fast. It handles sharing, gives you support during games, and runs mathematical simulations of your builds.

It's mobile-first and works the same on every major platform, so players can build and tweak lists anywhere.

Key features

Rules compliance

Built-in validation keeps every list within game rules and point limits.

Real-time sync

Lists stay up to date across every device automatically.

Five platforms

iOS, macOS, Android, Windows, and Linux, with the same experience everywhere.

Build simulations

Run the math on a list before you take it to the table.

Easy sharing

Send lists to your group or opponent in a couple of taps.

Offline-first

Works at the game store with no signal, and syncs when you're back online.

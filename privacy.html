Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

CodeSeed builds technology that serves people without extracting from them, and that applies to this website too. This page explains what information we collect on code-seed.io and what we do with it.

What we collect

We only collect what you choose to send us. When you fill in our contact form or sign up for the CodeSeed Studios newsletter, we receive the details you enter, such as your name, email address, company, and message.

This site doesn't use analytics, advertising trackers, or tracking cookies.

How we use it

We use your details only to reply to your inquiry, to talk with you about a project, or to send you the newsletter you signed up for. We never sell your information or share it for marketing.

Services we rely on

Form submissions are delivered to us through Formspree. The site loads fonts and icons from Google Fonts, Fontshare, and cdnjs, and its styling framework from the Tailwind CSS CDN. These providers may receive basic technical information, such as your IP address, when your browser loads those files.

Your choices

You can unsubscribe from the newsletter at any time, and you can ask us to show you, correct, or delete the information you've sent us. Just contact us through the form on our contact section.

Changes

If we change how we handle information, we'll update this page and the date above.

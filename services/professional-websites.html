The problem

Your website should match the quality of your work.

Most businesses face the same frustrating choice: pay for a cheap template that looks like everyone else's site, or commit to a full agency build that blows the budget. Templates are fast, but your clients can tell they're generic. Custom builds look great, but the cost and timeline are hard to justify when you're trying to grow.

There should be something in between. That's exactly what we built.

CodeSeed designs and develops professional websites with fully custom design, modern architecture, and a simple content dashboard, so you can update your own text, images, and pages without touching code or calling a developer.

How it works

From first call to launch in four steps.

  1. Step 1

    Discovery call

    We learn about your business, your audience, and what your website needs to do, then outline the scope and give you a clear quote.

  2. Step 2

    Design & build

    We design your site from scratch, with no templates or cookie-cutter layouts. Every page is crafted to match your brand.

  3. Step 3

    Content dashboard

    We set up a simple dashboard where you can update your own text, images, blog posts, team members, and more. No code needed.

  4. Step 4

    Launch & handoff

    We deploy your site, walk you through the dashboard, and hand you the keys. You own everything.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What if I don't have content ready?

We need your text and images before we start the design phase. If you need help with copywriting or photography, we can recommend partners, but content creation isn't included in the build.

What can I edit myself after launch?

Text, images, blog posts, team members, testimonials, portfolio items: basically any content. The layout, design, and styling are locked in so the site always looks professional.

What if I need changes to the design later?

Just reach out. We offer hourly support for anything beyond content edits, such as new pages, design tweaks, new features, or integrations. No retainer required.

How long does the whole process take?

Most projects are completed in 2–4 weeks from the time we receive your content. More complex sites may take longer, and we'll give you a clear timeline on our discovery call.

Do I really own the site?

Yes, 100%. The code, the design, and the content are all yours, on your own hosting account. If you ever want to move to a different developer, you can take everything with you.

What does hosting cost?

Typically about $10/month, paid directly to the hosting provider. We'll help you set it up during the build.

How does support work after launch?

We're available on an hourly basis, with no retainer or monthly commitment. You reach out when you need something, we give you a time estimate, and we bill for the work.

Ready for a website that represents your business?

Book a free call. Most calls take about 15 minutes, and we'll follow up with a written proposal within 48 hours.
